# SEA Rankforge design brief

Design read: SEA League players want a fast, transparent way to understand the
service, price, and next step without a loud or suspicious gaming storefront.

Concept spine: a rank board, moving from the current rank to the chosen result.

Delivery tier: cinema

Animation mode: animated-website — defaulted because the user did not answer
the animation intake question.

Locked palette: midnight navy #071326, ink #F4F7FF, cobalt #326BFF,
crimson #FF4D62, signal gold #F1C75B. This uses the supplied pricing artwork's
competitive esports language without copying its layout.

Locked type: Space Grotesk for display and Inter for utility/body copy. The
display face gives the rank board a technical, competitive voice; Inter keeps
pricing and form labels quick to scan.

Journey shape: single-shot. The supplied artwork is used as the visual anchor;
the clip holds the pricing board while the semantic chapters move from service
promise to transparent packages to a clear contact action.

Journey:
- The board appears: “Clear ranks. Clear prices.” Focus: the supplied service
  artwork. Proof tags: SEA only, GCash accepted.
- The price ladder: “Pick your climb.” Focus: rank and promotion packages.
  Proof tags: fixed PHP rates, duo +40%.
- The handoff: “Tell us where to start.” Focus: the contact path. Proof tags:
  no region selector, manual confirmation.

Camera architecture: A, centered editorial board with a slow forward drift.
Seam direction: not applicable for single-shot.
Mobile framing: keep the price board centered and let copy sit in the lower
third; the mobile source is the same centered-safe supplied artwork.

Section plan: journey, price table, process strip, trust and boundaries, contact
form, footer. Layout families alternate between cinematic overlay, dense price
matrix, horizontal process rail, split policy panel, and form panel.

Asset plan: supplied rank-pricing artwork as hero/poster/OG visual, generated
or locally encoded lightweight clip from that artwork, custom text-only mark,
and CSS rank tokens. No runtime media generation.

CTA inventory: “View prices” jumps to the price matrix; “Start an inquiry”
focuses the contact form; “Message on Facebook” opens the supplied Facebook
page. Each CTA has a separate visual treatment.
